### Slide 2: The Problem: The Recruiter's Dilemma
* **Pain Point 1**: **Recruiter Burnout**: Global BPOs and call centers conduct millions of repetitive 15-minute phone screens each year.
* **Pain Point 2**: **Legacy Tests are Broken**: Pearson Versant and static IVR exams test robotic repetition ("Repeat after me"). Candidates memorize test hacks, but freeze when real customers escalate.
* **Pain Point 3**: **High Attrition**: 45% of customer support agent turnover within the first 90 days stems from poor conversational fluency and stress management.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution: AcuityVoice
* **Core Innovation**: Move from static audio tests to **autonomous, multi-turn conversational roleplays**.
* **How It Works**:
  1. AI acts as an authentic customer with an urgent inquiry or dispute.
  2. Evaluates active listening, de-escalation composure, and CEFR spoken English level in real time.
  3. Delivers an objective, timestamped diagnostic scorecard within seconds.

---

### Slide 4: Technology: Powered by AssemblyAI Voice Agent API
* **Why AssemblyAI?**:
  * **Universal-3 Pro STT**: Accurate speech recognition across global accents and noisy headsets.
  * **Zero-Latency Turn-Taking & Barge-In**: Server-side VAD allows natural conversational interruption without awkward delays.
  * **Real-Time JSON-Schema Tool Calling**: Executes background linguistic analysis and customer sentiment updates concurrently while speaking.

### Slide 5: Product Walkthrough
* **Three Dynamic Components**:
  * **Live Recruiter View**: Real-time waveform, speaker diarization, and latency meter (<350ms).
  * **Dynamic Competency Radar**: Instant score updates across Fluency, Grammar, Vocabulary, Pronunciation, and Empathy.
  * **Customer Temperament Bar**: Visualizes emotional shift from *Hostile/Anxious* to *Calmed/Delighted*.
