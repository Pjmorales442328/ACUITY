What makes it reliable

Engineering decisions

  • The customer agent has no tools. Tool turns made it go silent mid-call; judging moved to a separate evaluator session.
  • Adaptive turn detection + max_accuracy. Nervous candidates who pause aren't cut off.
  • Evidence or nothing. Every finding is a verbatim quote with a timestamp; unmatched quotes are discarded.
  • Honest edge cases. Under 25 words of speech returns "Not enough speech", never an invented score.
Business value

A phone screen a reviewer can check in one minute.

  • Consistent: every candidate gets the same customer, the same rubric, the same metrics.
  • Evidence-backed: scores come with the candidate's own words and a jump-to-moment transcript.
  • Human in the loop: a readiness signal, not an automated hiring decision (hiring AI is high-risk under the EU AI Act).
